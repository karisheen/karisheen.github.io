Synthetic Health ML

Exploratory lung-cancer modeling on synthetic Synthea health records, developed collaboratively with Shawn-Keith Goforth.

PythonJupyterPandasDaskscikit-learnXGBoost

Inside the project.

  • Explored demographic and clinical-table joins across synthetic patient records, conditions, medications, and other health-related data.
  • Built Pandas and Dask preprocessing workflows and experimented with logistic regression, incremental classification, XGBoost, and SMOTE class balancing. Memory limits led to partitioned training on selected segments containing both target classes.
  • Investigated feature relevance through model coefficients and feature-importance visualizations.

SCOPE / CONTEXT

What this work represents.

Research on synthetic data, not a diagnostic tool or a clinically validated prediction system. The exploratory evaluation has methodological limitations, so no accuracy or clinical-impact claims are presented here.
