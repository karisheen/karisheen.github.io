A crypto prediction-market research pipeline combining Kalshi, Coinbase, and Deribit inputs with probability models, walk-forward backtesting, and an auditable SQLite record.

Method and evidence

Point-in-time replay models fees and liquidity-constrained fills. The system records how inputs become estimates so a result can be examined after the fact.

Research only: no trade execution or validated trading edge.
