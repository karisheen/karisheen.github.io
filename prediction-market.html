01 PROBLEM / RESPONSIBILITY

A probability is not evidence.

The central problem was not generating a forecast. It was preserving what the system knew at decision time, keeping dense contracts from overstating independent evidence, and preventing a plausible model output from bypassing fees, liquidity, uncertainty, or exposure constraints.

I designed and implemented the data adapters, contract semantics, probability models, walk-forward replay, calibration gates, risk policy, audit store, command-line workflows, and operating checks as an independent engineering project.

03 ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Confidence is earned, not inherited.

DECISION 01

Fail closed on semantics.

Contracts are parsed into explicit range, threshold, or touch-barrier structures. Ambiguous resolution rules do not receive a forecast.

DECISION 02

Group evidence by event.

One outcome-weighted sample per structural model prevents hundreds of mutually exclusive ladder contracts from masquerading as independent outcomes.

DECISION 03

Bind approval to identity.

Model version, recipe fingerprint, data cadence, and deployment policy are separate identities. A profile fitted for different behavior cannot approve live delivery.

DECISION 04

Make SQLite authoritative.

Forecasts, exact input context, rejections, delivery state, and immutable run manifests remain inspectable even when Discord is disabled.

04 VERIFICATION / EVIDENCE

Replay before approval. Observe before claiming.

  • No-look-ahead replayOnly outcomes settled by each training cutoff can calibrate the next non-overlapping test window.
  • Conservative execution modelSignals use executable quotes; fills use a later adverse candle extreme, whole contracts, and volume participation caps.
  • Event-grouped calibrationProbability and horizon regions carry held-out uncertainty; sparse or unsupported regions fail closed.
  • Reproducible manifestsEvery run records git revision, source hashes, dataset boundaries, model recipe, engine configuration, and campaign identity.
  • Forward shadow ledgerScheduled scans retain forecasts and reasons without sending an alert or executing a trade.
05 LIMITATIONS / NEXT EVIDENCE

A research baseline, not a validated edge.

The system is operationally focused on KXBTC and remains shadow-first. Candlestick volume constrains participation but cannot reconstruct order-book queue position or an exact fill path. Entry candidates are observations, not permission to trade.

Broader historical coverage, untouched forward observations across multiple regimes, independent review, and a separate execution and position-management system would be required before any real-money use.
